A personal assistant that stays at home.

claw-assistant is a private, single-user personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It is not a public service and does not accept sign-ups.

Organises email

Reads and organises the owner's own email, with the owner's permission.

Sends only on approval

Sends an email only after the owner has reviewed and approved it.

Its own calendar

Manages a separate calendar the app itself created, and nothing else.

Data is handled on the owner's own computer and is never sold, shared or used for advertising. Read exactly what is accessed and why in the privacy policy.
