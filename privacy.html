Privacy policy

Your data stays with you.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

claw-assistant is a personal tool used by a single person (its owner) on their own computer. It is not offered to the public.

1Data accessed

  • Gmail (read): messages and attachments, used only to carry out tasks the owner asks for.
  • Gmail (send): used only to send an email the owner has reviewed and explicitly approved.
  • Google Calendar: limited to a secondary calendar created by this app. The app cannot see or change any other calendar.

2How data is used and stored

Data is processed on the owner's own computer. Only the minimum needed is stored there. Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else. No Google user data is sent to third-party servers or used to train AI models.

3Google API Services

claw-assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4Retention and deletion

Locally stored data is kept only while useful and can be deleted by the owner at any time. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which the app can no longer access the account.

5Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on this app's Google sign-in screen.
